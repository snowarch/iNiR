# Editorial style

Editorial is a global style for iNiR. It gives the shell paper-like surfaces, a strong focal card, large headlines and small, quiet labels. It works with Material and Waffle.

## Turn it on

- Material: **Settings › Themes › Style › Global Style**, then pick **Editorial**.
- Waffle: **Waffle Settings › Themes › Global Style**, then pick **Editorial**. Waffle keeps its own layouts and takes Editorial's colours and type.

Once Editorial is the global style, its editor appears under **Settings › Themes › Style › Editorial**. Waffle uses the same values, so tune them there.

## Presets

At the top of the editor, a preset sets many options at once:

- **Poster** uses bold sans headlines.
- **Studio** is compact and quiet. It adds the second paper layer and a paper tint in your primary colour, with smaller headlines and corners.
- **Reading** uses serif headlines, with a little more space and rounder corners.

**Reset Editorial** puts everything back to the defaults: one sheet of paper that follows your theme.

## Paper

Under **Material**:

- **Paper mode** picks **Follow theme**, **Light paper** or **Charcoal**.
- **Paper pigment** picks **Neutral**, **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Tertiary** or **Custom**. The theme colours change with your theme. Custom stays fixed.
- **Paper tint** sets how strongly the pigment colours the paper and the highlighted cards. **Paper warmth** warms the sheet.
- **Second paper layer** adds a backing sheet under the main surfaces, and **Layer depth** sets how far it shows. Small controls stay flat.
- **Glass background** frosts the wallpaper behind the paper. **Paper opacity** and **Backdrop blur** set how much of it shows through.

## Type and accent

Under **Voice & ink**:

- **Poster sans** or **Reading serif** picks the headline face.
- **Title scale** sizes headings. Body text does not change.
- **Headline weight** and **Headline tracking** set how heavy and how spaced headings are.
- **Label weight** and **Metadata tracking** set the small labels and captions.
- **Accent pigment** picks where highlights come from: **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Tertiary** or **Custom**.
- **Accent intensity** moves from neutral ink to richer, theme-coloured highlights and selection fields.

## Spacing and motion

Under **Composition**:

- **Spacing** sets the gaps between sections and cards.
- **Corner radius** rounds cards and compact controls together.
- **Editorial motion** scales animation speed. Reduced motion still wins.
- **Flower accents** shows the small Flower mark at a few spots. Turn it off to hide them.

## Settings in the Editorial layout

Settings can open as an Editorial page too. Turn on **Settings › Modules › Settings UI › Overlay mode (live preview)**, then choose **Overlay layout › Editorial**. It keeps the same pages and search.
