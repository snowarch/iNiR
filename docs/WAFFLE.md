# Waffle

Waffle is laid out like Windows 11: a taskbar along the bottom, a Start menu, an Action Center and a Notification
Center. Everything here is set in **Waffle Settings**, which opens with **Super+,**.

<!-- preview waffle taskbar,start choose=apps,taskbar -->

## On your screen

- **Taskbar**: along the bottom, or the top. **Waffle Settings › Taskbar** sets its icons, the clock, Desktop Peek and
  the update badge.
- **Start menu**: search, pinned apps and recommendations. **Waffle Settings › Waffle Style › Start Menu** sets its
  layout.
- **Action Center**: quick toggles, volume and brightness.
- **Notification Center**: the notification history and the calendar.
- **Widgets panel**: weather, media, the calendar and the widgets you add from the Hub.
- **Task View**: every window and workspace.

## Where things are in Waffle Settings

Pages are listed on the left: Quick, General, Taskbar, Background, Themes, Gowall, Interface, Modules, Waffle Style,
Shortcuts, About, Monitors, Autostart, Workspace Strip, Mascot, AI, Effects, Shell Layout and Hub.
**Ctrl+PageDown** and **Ctrl+PageUp** move between them, and **Ctrl+F** searches every option.

- **General**: audio, battery, time and language, idle and sleep, Game Mode and the calendar.
- **Background**: the wallpaper, one per monitor, transitions, effects and the overview backdrop.
- **Interface**: display scaling, notifications, the floating tools (**Super+G**), the on-screen display and the lock
  screen.
- **Waffle Style**: Alt+Tab, Task View, Start, the Widgets panel, weather and the calendar.
- **Gowall**: recolours an image with your palette, pixelates or upscales it.

## Its looks

**Waffle Settings › Themes** picks the colour theme and the global style. Waffle keeps its own layouts and takes the
style's colours and type. Colours follow the wallpaper unless you pick a theme: [Colours and themes](THEMING_PRESETS.md).
